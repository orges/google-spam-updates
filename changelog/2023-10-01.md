## October 2023 Spam Update

```text
+ Wednesday, October 4, 2023
  We're releasing an update to our spam detections systems today that will improve our coverage in
   many languages and spam types.
  This spam update aims to clean up several types of spam that our community members reported in
   Turkish, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Hindi, Chinese, and other languages. We expect this to reduce
   the visible spam in search results, particularly when it comes to cloaking, hacked, auto-
   generated, and scraped spam. The update will take a few weeks to roll out.
  Sites that see a change after a spam update should review our spam policies . Learn more on our
   Google Search spam updates and your site help page.
  We are grateful to our Help community and users who sent us spam reports for their valuable
   feedback of spam trends in various languages. These reports helped us prioritize and validate our
   solutions. We're already working on our next update to further protect your search experience and
   we appreciate your continued support! Please continue to participate in the community (which is
   available in multiple languages!) and send us feedback through our newly-improved spam report
   form .
  Posted by Duy Nguyen , Search Quality Analyst
```
