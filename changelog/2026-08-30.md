   site is subject to a manual action under this policy outside the EEA isn't used as a signal for
   ranking that content within the EEA.

+## A portion of my site was previously subject to manual action under this policy in the EEA.
   What happens now?
+Google will lift all previous manual actions taken under this policy for pages appearing in
